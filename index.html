Product approach

How I make product decisions.

The common thread is the ability to find clarity in systems where users, data, policy, and commercial outcomes all pull in different directions.

01

AI should earn its place

I use AI when it improves a decision, removes real effort, or creates measurable business value, not as a decorative feature.

02

Design the ecosystem

In platform products, one role's action becomes another role's dependency. I design permissions, handoffs, incentives, and exceptions together.

03

Make regulation usable

Good RegTech turns policy into understandable flows, visible controls, and sensible defaults without pushing complexity onto the user.

04

Measure both sides

I connect user outcomes such as speed and clarity with business outcomes such as activation, efficiency, adoption, and revenue opportunity.
